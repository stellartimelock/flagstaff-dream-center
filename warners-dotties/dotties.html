A cup brewed just for you, among the plants

Dottie’s sits right in the heart of Warner’s Nursery. Grab your favorite coffee, tea or Italian soda and take a well-deserved break on our patio, or stroll the nursery with your drink as you shop for houseplants, trees, shrubs and flowers.

Named for Dottie

Dottie (Dorothy) Warner started the family’s first plant and gift shop in Page, Arizona. When her granddaughters opened a coffee shop inside the Flagstaff nursery, there was no doubt what the name would be.
