Free fall fun

Starting in late September, our always-free pumpkin patch and hay maze are open, with carving and “fancy” pumpkins for sale, free games and a pumpkin decorating station. We also host family events through the year, including an Easter Egg Hunt and Fall Festival.

Our story

The story starts with Bill Warner and Dottie, who moved to Page, Arizona in the 1950s. Dottie started a plant and gift shop there, and the couple’s three sons grew the nursery into a landscape company too.

Warner’s opened in Flagstaff in 1990 on North Highway 89, then moved to the larger Butler Avenue location in 1995. Dottie’s granddaughters, Misti Warner-Andersen and Marni Montague, now lead the team, and the fourth generation helps out in summer.

Dottie’s influence is still everywhere: in the “Dot’s Back Porch” gift shop and in the coffee shop that bears her name. Meet Dottie’s →
