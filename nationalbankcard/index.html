Clover Go: take card payments on your phone

Clover Go pairs the Clover Go app with a portable Bluetooth card reader, so your phone becomes a point of sale wherever you do business. National Bankcard offers Clover Go alongside its other mobile card readers.

  • Works with iOS and Android. Use the phone or tablet you already have; the app is also optimized for iPad screens.
  • Take all tender types. Accept major credit and debit cards by chip or contactless, including Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay, plus Tap to Pay on iPhone in the Clover Go app.
  • Digital receipts. Send receipts by email or text.
  • Tips, refunds and open orders. One-touch tipping, full or partial refunds, and orders you can open, edit and pay later.
  • WiFi or cellular. Take payments anywhere you have an internet connection, all synced to your Clover Dashboard.
