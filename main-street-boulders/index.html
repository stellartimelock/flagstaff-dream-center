How 24/7 works

Getting your key fob

  • Buy an MSB Weekly or Monthly membership. The first week or month is $0
  • Contact the gym to switch to Premium, then follow the steps they send
  • Fob is $10 and ready within 48 hrs on business days (no weekend or holiday setups)
  • Dues start once your fob is ready. If you don't finish Premium setup, regular dues start after the first week or month

After-hours rules

  • Premium members only. Your fob is for you only, one person per entry
  • Scan the fob and look up at the camera. No need to scan out. Secure the door when you leave
  • No staff on duty, so climbing with a partner is strongly encouraged. There's a phone at the front desk for emergencies
  • Under 18 only with a parent or guardian on the same Premium membership
  • Guests must stay with their member host. Misuse can end your membership

Member benefits

  • 1 free guest pass per month (rental gear included). Works during open hours and after hours. Guests can use the self-serve kiosk
  • 15% off in the Downtown Crag gear shop (food and drink excluded), plus member-only sales
  • 20% off climbing instruction. Select free clinics, programs and group fitness classes
  • 50% off next month's dues for helping at a Northern Arizona Climbers Coalition trail day

Both gyms: DTC + MSB membership · same-day passes. Members: Mindbody login. For changes, email membership@flagstaffclimbing.com at least 7 days before billing. Full terms.

First visit no experience needed
  1. Sign the waiver online or at the desk (a parent or guardian signs for under 18s).
  2. Come Tue–Thu 6–10 pm in athletic clothes. Shoes rent for $5.
  3. Check in at the front desk and get your day pass.

Gym rules
  • Per gym rules, climbers must pass a proficiency test or take a lesson by staff before using the facility
  • Kids under 12 need an adult with them. Climbers of any age are welcome
  • No barefoot climbing. No food or drink on the pads. Gear goes in the cubbies
  • No smoking, alcohol or drugs. Watch for falling climbers
