Hand-made crepes on a downtown patio

Sweet and savory crepes: a creative take on the French classic with a New Jersey flair, served on an open-air patio next to Heritage Square.

Made just for you

Every crepe is made to order while you watch, then handed over in a paper cone that's easy to eat at a table or on a walk around downtown. Choose sweet, like strawberry cheesecake or Nutella and berries, or savory, like ham and cheddar or spinach and artichoke dip.
