Peace, Love, and Crepes

Hand-made sweet and savory crepes: a creative take on the French classic with a New Jersey flair, served on an open-air patio next to Heritage Square.

See the menuHours & directions

Made just for you

Every crepe is made to order while you watch, then handed over in a paper cone that's easy to eat at a table or on a walk around downtown. Choose sweet, like roasted apple and caramel or Nutella and berries, or savory, like ham and mozzarella or spinach and artichoke.

  • Outdoor patio: covered seating in the alley by the Old Town Shops
  • Dog-friendly: bring the whole crew
  • Vegetarian & gluten-free options: just ask at the counter
  • Sunday farmers' market: find us there, 8 am to noon
