Custom cakes

How custom cake orders work

  • 48 hours notice is required for custom cakes.
  • Starting prices include cake and basic buttercream design: texture, borders, writing and sprinkles.
  • Additional decoration has a decoration charge and needs a custom quote.
  • Tiered cakes need a custom quote. Please call or email.
  • Every flavor is available gluten free, vegan or both.
